Rules & Scoring

From the league sheet

General

  1. Rosters total no more than 18 players. Fewer than 18 is allowed.
  2. Start 1 quarterback, 1 kicker, 1 defense, at least 1 running back, and any combination of wide receivers and tight ends to fill 9 starting positions.
  3. The draft runs after the final preseason game and before the first regular season game.
  4. Lineups are due by 12:00 Sunday afternoon. Thursday and Saturday players must be called in before game time that day. Last week's lineup stands unless you change it.
  5. Each team gets 5 transactions per season — a roster addition or a trade. Trades have no player limit.
  6. Nobody is added or dropped until the commissioner is called. First come, first served.
  7. No transactions after Week 12.
  8. Weekly ties stand. The scoring system depends on the number of teams and is announced on draft night.
  9. All decisions made by the commissioner are FINAL.

Scoring — Offense

  1. Touchdown run scores 6.
  2. Touchdown pass scores 3 for the passer and 3 for the receiver.
  3. Kicks are scored accordingly.
  4. Passing yards, in 20-yard steps:
    260–279 = 1 · 280–299 = 2 · 300–319 = 3
    320–339 = 4 · 340–359 = 5 · 360–379 = 6
    380–399 = 7 · and so on 460–479 = 11
  5. Rushing yards, in 20-yard steps:
    60–79 = 1 · 80–99 = 2 · 100–119 = 3
    120–139 = 4 · 140–159 = 5 · 160–179 = 6
    180–199 = 7 · 200–219 = 8 · and so on
  6. Receiving yards score the same as rushing yards, plus receptions:
    5 rec = 1 · 6 rec = 2 · 7 rec = 3
    8 rec = 4 · 9 rec = 5 · and so on 14 rec = 10

Scoring — Defense

  1. Any turnover recovered by the defense scores 1.
  2. A turnover returned for a touchdown scores 7.
  3. Special teams turnovers and the touchdowns that come directly from them score for the defense.
  4. Any safety scores for the defense.
  5. Rushing yards allowed:
    80–99 = 1 · 60–79 = 2 · 40–59 = 3
    20–39 = 4 · 1–19 = 5 · 0 or negative = 6
  6. Passing yards allowed:
    180–199 = 1 · 160–179 = 2 · 140–159 = 3
    120–139 = 4 · 100–119 = 5 · 80–99 = 6
    60–79 = 7 · and so on
  7. Points allowed:
    14–16 = 1 · 11–13 = 2 · 8–10 = 3
    5–7 = 4 · 2–4 = 5 · shutout = 6

Where the sheet is silent — what the auto-scorer assumes

The league sheet doesn't spell these out, so the feed makes a call. Change them at the top of the script (HOUSE) or just overwrite the number on the sheet — a typed number always beats the feed.

Kicks are scored accordingly → field goal 3, extra point 1. Nothing extra for distance.
Safety → 2 to the defense, on top of the turnover and yardage lines.
Two-point conversions → 2 to the scorer; a two-point pass is 1 to the passer and 1 to the receiver.
Interceptions and lost fumbles by your own players → no penalty. The sheet lists no negative, so none is applied.
Rushing and receiving yards → scored on separate ladders, then added. A back with 70 rushing and 70 receiving gets 1 + 1, not the 3 he'd get if you pooled 140 yards.
Passing yards under 260 → 0. The ladder starts at 260.
Points allowed 17 or more → 0. The ladder tops out at 14–16.
1 point allowed → treated as the 2–4 band. The sheet skips it.
